About WebAble

WebAble is a web accessibility analyzer built as a BSc Computer Science final year project. It fetches a public web page, parses its HTML and reports common barriers for people who use screen readers or keyboards.

Technology

  • Python & Flask
  • BeautifulSoup & Requests
  • SQLite with hashed passwords

Automated checks cover only part of WCAG. Always combine them with manual testing.
